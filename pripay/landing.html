How it works

Private payroll in four steps

PriPay pays your team through Starknet's privacy pool — private on-chain, fully transparent on your side.

1

Connect your wallet

Sign in with Ready, Braavos, MetaMask or ForDefi. PriPay never holds your keys or your viewing key.

2

Add your team

Import people from a CSV or add them one by one, with salaries set in ILS or USD and converted at the official daily rate.

3

Run payroll privately

Pay everyone in one click. Transfers route through Starknet's privacy pool, so amounts and recipients never appear on block explorers.

4

Keep a tax-ready trail

Every payment stays private on-chain but fully visible to you: full history, CSV export and per-payment invoices for your accountant.
